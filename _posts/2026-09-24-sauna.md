### Discovering autologon credentials

## Privilege Escalation

I had already collected BloodHound data, so I inspected `svc_loanmgr`.

## Conclusion

The attack chain was: anonymous web access exposed usernames, AS-REP roasting recovered `fsmith`, WinPEAS revealed the `svc_loanmgr` password, and DCSync rights exposed the Administrator hash.
