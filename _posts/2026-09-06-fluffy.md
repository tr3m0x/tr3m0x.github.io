After importing the data into BloodHound, the important path started with `p.agila`'s group memberships.

![group](/assets/img/posts/fluffy/group.png)

The user `p.agila` is a member of `Service Account Managers`, which has `GenericAll` over the `Service Accounts` group. That means I can modify the group, including adding my own user to it.

The `Service Accounts` group has `GenericWrite` over `ca_svc`, `ldap_svc`, and `winrm_svc`. After adding `p.agila` to `Service Accounts`, those write permissions become directly useful.

## Exploitation

I focused on `winrm_svc` because the account had remote management access through WinRM. since we have `GenericWrite` over the user object, we can perform a shadow credentials attack to retrieve the NT hash and authenticate as that user.

## Privilege Escalation

### AD CS Enumeration

BloodHound also showed that `ca_svc` was one of the service accounts I could modify.

I used the same shadow credentials technique against `ca_svc` to retrieve its NT hash:

```text
ca_svc:ca0f4f9e9eb8a092addf53bb03fc98c8
```

The account name and SPN suggested AD CS was in play, so I enumerated certificate services with Certipy.

```bash
tr3m0x@ubuntu:~/htb/windows/fluffy$ certipy find -u "ca_svc@fluffy.htb" -hashes ca0f4f9e9eb8a092addf53bb03fc98c8 -vulnerable -stdout -ns 10.129.58.158 -target 10.129.58.158
Certipy v5.1.0 - by Oliver Lyak (ly4k)
...
    [!] Vulnerabilities
      ESC16                             : Security Extension is disabled.
    [*] Remarks
      ESC16                             : Other prerequisites may be required for this to be exploitable. See the wiki for more details.
Certificate Templates                   : [!] Could not find any certificate templates
```

The CA was vulnerable to **ESC16**, meaning the AD CS security extension is disabled globally. Without the security extension, issued certificates do not contain the strong SID binding that normally prevents this style of identity confusion. I used this poc [ESC16 - Security Extension Disabled on CA Globally](https://github.com/ly4k/Certipy/wiki/06-%E2%80%90-Privilege-Escalation#esc16-security-extension-disabled-on-ca-globally).
